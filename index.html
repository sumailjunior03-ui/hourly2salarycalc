What This Calculator Does

This hourly to salary calculator converts an hourly wage into weekly, monthly, and annual salary estimates.

You enter an hourly rate and your typical working schedule (hours per week and weeks per year). The calculator estimates gross pay across different time periods for planning and comparison.

Results are pre-tax estimates only. Taxes, deductions, overtime, and benefits are not included.

Example Hourly to Salary Conversion

  • Hourly rate: $25.00
  • Hours per week: 40
  • Weeks per year: 52

Annual estimate: $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000 per year

Derived estimates:

  • Monthly: $52,000 ÷ 12 ≈ $4,333
  • Bi-weekly: $52,000 ÷ 26 = $2,000
  • Weekly: $52,000 ÷ 52 = $1,000
  • Daily (8-hour day): $25 × 8 = $200

Formula

Annual Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours per Week × Weeks per Year

Derived values:

  • Monthly = Annual ÷ 12
  • Bi-weekly = Annual ÷ 26
  • Weekly = Annual ÷ 52
  • Daily (8-hour day) = Hourly Rate × 8

Calculation Methodology

  1. The calculator takes your hourly rate, hours per week, and weeks per year.
  2. It estimates annual gross salary as: hourly × hours per week × weeks per year.
  3. It derives monthly pay from the annual value (annual ÷ 12).
  4. It derives bi-weekly pay from the annual value (annual ÷ 26).
  5. It derives weekly pay from the annual value (annual ÷ 52).
  6. It estimates daily pay using an 8-hour day assumption (hourly × 8).

This tool provides gross estimates only. It does not account for taxes, overtime rules, paid or unpaid time off, or employer benefits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this include overtime?

No. This calculator converts a base hourly rate using the hours and weeks you enter. Overtime rules vary by employer and location and are not factored in.

Does this include taxes?

No. All results are gross estimates before taxes, deductions, and benefits. Use a payroll calculator for net-pay estimates.

Why do I need weeks per year?

Weeks per year affects the annual estimate because unpaid time off, contract schedules, or seasonal work can reduce working weeks below 52.

How is daily pay calculated?

Daily pay is estimated using a standard 8-hour day assumption: hourly rate × 8. This is independent of your hours-per-week setting.

Is this exact?

No. Results are estimates for comparison and planning. Actual pay varies with holidays, time off, and employer payroll policies.
