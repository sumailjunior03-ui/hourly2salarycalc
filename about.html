About Hourly2SalaryCalc.com

Hourly2SalaryCalc.com is a free tool that converts any hourly wage into annual, monthly, bi-weekly, weekly, and daily salary equivalents. It accounts for the hours you actually work and the weeks you actually get paid — because real schedules don't always follow the 40 × 52 default.

Who this is for

Anyone comparing hourly pay to a salary offer, budgeting on an hourly wage, or trying to understand what their rate means in annual terms. Also useful for freelancers, contractors, and part-time workers converting between pay structures.

How it works

Enter your hourly rate, hours per week, and weeks per year. The calculator produces salary equivalents for every common pay period. Adjust the hours and weeks to match your actual schedule — whether that's 37.5 hours, 50 weeks, or something else entirely.
